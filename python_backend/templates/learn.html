What PCOS does to the ovaries

Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal condition. The ovaries often hold many small follicles that do not mature and release an egg, which is why cycles become irregular.

Common signs

  • Irregular or missed periods
  • Weight gain, especially around the waist
  • Excess facial or body hair
  • Acne or oily skin
  • Dark, velvety patches of skin

When to see a doctor

Book an appointment if your periods are irregular for several months, if you are trying to conceive without success, or if several of these signs appear together. A doctor confirms PCOS with blood tests and an ultrasound.
