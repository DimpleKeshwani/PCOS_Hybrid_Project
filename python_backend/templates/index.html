Frequently asked questions

Is this a diagnosis?

No. It estimates how similar your answers are to PCOS patients in a research dataset. Only a doctor can diagnose PCOS, usually with blood tests and an ultrasound.

How accurate is it?

In testing, about 82% accuracy using symptoms alone and about 90% when ultrasound follicle counts are added. It still misses some cases and raises some false alarms.

Is my data stored?

Your answers are sent to the model only to calculate the result. This demo does not save them. The "near me" search runs in your browser and your location is not sent to this site's server.

What data was the model trained on?

A public dataset of 541 women from 10 hospitals in Kerala, India. Results may differ for other populations.
